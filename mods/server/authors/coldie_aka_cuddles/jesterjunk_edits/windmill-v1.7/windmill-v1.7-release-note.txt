windmill-v1.7

release note:

updated concrete recipe for windmill




Old concrete recipe

  1 clay           =   2000g  =   2kg
  1 rock shards    =  20000g  =  20kg
-------------------------------------
  1 concrete, clay =   3000g  =   3kg


 10 clay           =  20000g  =  20kg
 10 rock shards    = 200000g  = 200kg
-------------------------------------
 10 concrete, clay =  30000g  =  30kg




New concrete recipe (Windmill only)

  0.5 clay         =   1000g  =   1kg
  0.1 rock shards  =   2000g  =   2kg
-------------------------------------
  1 concrete, clay =   3000g  =   3kg


  5 clay           =  10000g  =  10kg
  1 rock shards    =  20000g  =  20kg
-------------------------------------
 10 concrete, clay =  30000g  =  30kg


100 clay           = 200000g  = 200kg
 20 rock shards    = 400000g  = 400kg
-------------------------------------
200 concrete, clay = 600000g  = 600kg
